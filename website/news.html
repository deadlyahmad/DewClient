DEWCOSMETICS · 0.4.0October 9, 2026

DewCosmetics 0.4.0: cosmetics across five Minecraft versions

DewCosmetics 0.4.0 adds Fabric support for Minecraft 1.21.4, 1.21.8, 1.21.11, 26.2, and 26.3. Supported modded clients can sync skins, capes, and wings across these versions. Free cape claims are available to Microsoft and Discord accounts, not offline sandbox profiles.
