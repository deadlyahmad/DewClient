Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy describes information DewClient processes when you use the Windows launcher, its account and cosmetics services, and dewclient.fun. DewClient is operated by the DewClient project team (“DewClient”, “we”, or “us”).

1. Sign-in and account identity

Microsoft sign-in is completed through Microsoft’s official authentication flow. DewClient receives the account tokens needed to verify the selected Minecraft profile and launch the game; DewClient does not ask for or store your Microsoft password.

Discord sign-in uses Discord OAuth with the identify permission. A Discord profile is a separate DewClient identity with its own in-game name. Linking Discord to a Microsoft Minecraft profile verifies your membership in the DewClient community and enables eligible claims; it does not provide access to the other provider’s account or credentials. Offline profiles are stored on your device and do not receive account-linked cloud claims.

2. Information processed

  • Account and player details: provider identifiers, in-game name, Minecraft UUID, avatar, and account type needed to provide sign-in, profile, and linking features.
  • Launcher connection: a device identifier and client version; when connected features are enabled, profile names, UUIDs, account types, and recent-use details may be sent to the DewClient server. The active Microsoft player’s name and UUID may be shown in the online-player feature while the launcher is running.
  • Cosmetics: cape claim and selection records, assigned cosmetic identifiers, and cosmetic image files you choose to upload. Cosmetic files are stored by the service to make the selected appearance available to your game and other DewClient players.
  • Support: information you choose to include in a bug report, such as a description and relevant diagnostic details.
  • Website: basic request and security information processed by the website host and network provider when a page or download is requested.

3. How information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, launch Minecraft, manage profile names and Discord links, process cosmetic claims and assignments, sync cosmetics, secure the service, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information or use Microsoft credentials for advertising.

4. Service providers and disclosure

Microsoft and Discord authenticate their respective accounts. Supabase provides database and authentication infrastructure; our server and domain providers deliver the API, website, and downloads. Those providers process information as needed to operate their services. We disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

5. Storage and retention

Account, linking, claim, and cosmetic records are retained while needed to operate your profile and the service. Online presence expires after the launcher stops sending heartbeats. Local launcher data remains on your device until you remove it or reset the launcher. Some security and operational records may remain for a limited period.

6. Your choices

You can remove local profiles and reset launcher data from the launcher. You can stop using connected services by signing out or uninstalling the launcher. For requests about cloud account or cosmetic records, contact support@dewclient.fun from the account you control so we can verify the request.

7. Children and legal rights

DewClient is not directed to children below the minimum age required to use the connected account services in their region. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, subject to applicable law and service security requirements.

8. Security and changes

We use access controls and transport security appropriate to the service, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy as the product changes. The date above shows when this version took effect.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@dewclient.fun or the DewClient Discord community.

DewClient is an independent third-party project and is not affiliated with Microsoft, Mojang Studios, or Discord.
